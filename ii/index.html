WHY OROMIA? _

Why you choose Oromia as your investment destnation?

Oromia has become a top investment destination due to its vast natural resources, fertile land, young workforce, strategic location near Addis Ababa, and investor-friendly policies, offering unmatched opportunities in agriculture, manufacturing, mining, energy, and tourism sectors with strong government support.

Access to major transport corridors, including the Addis-Djibouti railway, enhances logistics and international market connectivity.

Competitive incentives like tax holidays, land access, and streamlined licensing make investment easy and cost-effective.

Diverse agro-ecological zones support year-round agricultural production, ideal for agribusiness and agro-processing industries and industrial Parks.

Agriculture & Agro-Processing

Agriculture in Oromia

Oromia is the agricultural heartland of Ethiopia, offering fertile land, diverse climates, and abundant water resources. The region leads in coffee, cereals, oilseeds, fruits, and vegetables with investors welcomed by land access, irrigation support, and agro-industry linkage opportunities.

  • World-class coffee production & export
  • Large rural workforce & agro-processing
  • Government-backed irrigation programs
  • Export-oriented farming incentives

Manufacturing in Oromia

Oromia provides a strategic landscape for manufacturing investment, driven by abundant raw materials, industrial zones, and a skilled workforce. Industrial parks in Adama, Jimma, and other cities offer ready infrastructure and reliable utilities for scalable industrial growth.

Tourism & Eco-Tourism

Tourism in Oromia

Oromia boasts a rich blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and historical landmarks. From the Bale Mountains to Sof Omar Caves and scenic lakes like Ziway and Langano, the region offers diverse eco, adventure, and cultural tourism opportunities.

  • Bale Mountains & Sof Omar Caves
  • Lakes Ziway, Langano & scenic trails
  • Eco-tourism & cultural experiences

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as an investor in Oromia?

Complete the online application form above, upload required documents, and submit. Our team will contact you within 3 working days to confirm receipt and next steps.

What documents do I need?

Valid passport or national ID, business plan, capital source evidence, and sector-specific licenses. Download our complete checklist from the Resources section.

How long does the approval process take?

Standard applications take 5–15 working days. Complex or large-scale projects may require additional board review time. Track your application in real time using our tracker.

Are there minimum capital requirements?

For domestic investors, the minimum is generally 150,000 ETB. For foreign investors, it is USD 200,000 for most sectors. Some sectors have specific requirements — check sector guides.

Can I invest in multiple sectors?

Yes. Investors may apply for multiple investment licenses across different sectors, subject to each sector's requirements and capital availability.
